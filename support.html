Common questions

How do I steer? Drag on the left half of the screen. The slider on the right is throttle (up) and brake (down).

How do I pit? Tap BOX during a race, then tap once in the pit box to change all four tyres.

Why must I pit? In races of three laps or more, each car makes one stop. The game tells you which lap is yours.

Can I change the camera? Tap the camera icon to cycle chase, TV, cockpit and high views.

Where is my save? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

Is there sound? Yes. Turn off silent mode, and check the volume sliders in Settings.
