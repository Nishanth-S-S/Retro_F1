FORMULA RETRO

Privacy Policy · Last updated 7 November 2026

The short version

Formula Retro does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the game keeps

Your career, Road to Glory progress, settings and best times are saved only on your own device. They never leave it, and deleting the app deletes them.

What the game does not do

It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. It makes no network requests to us or to third parties, and it contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.

Children

Because no data is collected, the game is safe for players of any age.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version is released.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the Formula Retro GitHub page.
