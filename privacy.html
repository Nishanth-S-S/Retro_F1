FORMULA RETRO

Privacy Policy · Last updated 8 November 2026

Summary

Formula Retro does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your own device. They are never sent anywhere, and deleting the app deletes them.

Data we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not connect to the internet.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code.

Children

Because no data is collected, the game is suitable for players of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated before the new version of the game is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on the Formula Retro GitHub page.
